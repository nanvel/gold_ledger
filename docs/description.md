# Description

GoldLedger is a specialized accounting SaaS designed for gold suppliers and retailers,
streamlining transactions and financial tracking.

Suppliers can enter products given including payment information,
while retailers have the flexibility to accept or reject them.
Retailers can enter payments, which suppliers can approve or decline.

The platform features a comprehensive balance sheet that provides real-time insights into outstanding payments, 
due dates, and overall financial status, ensuring seamless cash flow management for businesses in the gold trade.

## Title

Streamlining transactions and financial tracking for businesses in the gold trade.

## How it works

Register -> Show QR code to connect -> Create a product / payment

## Scenario

- Suppliers registers
- Supplier invites a retailer (register + invite)
- Supplier adds a product
- Product pending acceptance is visible
- Retailer accepts the product
- Pending payment is visible in the balance sheet
- Retailer creates a payment
- Supplier accepts the payment
- No pending payment is visible
